Desert Island List 🌴

This page contains some thinkers and texts that have shaped how I approach literature, culture, and history or made a particularly strong impression on me more generally, regardless of whether they are explicitly cited in my published work. It is not exhaustive and the entries are listed randomly (not in order of their perceived importance). Given my concentration in decadence studies, I have refrained from including any decadent works here or from my book project (though some may still have tangential connections to decadence!)


Poetry

My lover comes to me like darkfall—long,

and through my open window. Mullion, transom.

A good window lets the outside participate.

I keep time on the hematite clocks of her shoulders.

And I’ve done so much of it—time.

— Natalie Diaz, Like Church.

Perhaps you will spend your life there, and never see Chicago again. And you will, I assure you, as long as space and time divide you from anyone you love, discover a great deal about shipping routes, airlines, earthquake, famine, disease, and war. And you will always know what time it is in Hong Kong, for you love someone who lives there...
